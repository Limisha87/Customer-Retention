CUSTOMER RETENTION STRATEGIES

1. HIGH CHURN RISK CUSTOMERS

Customers identified as high churn risk should receive proactive
communication from the customer support or retention team.

The company should identify the main reasons for churn and provide
a personalized solution.

Possible actions include:
- Proactive customer support
- Personalized offers
- Service improvement
- Contract incentives
- Dedicated support
- Follow-up communication


2. NEW CUSTOMERS

Customers with short tenure may have a higher risk of churn because
they have not yet developed a strong relationship with the service.

Recommended actions:
- Provide onboarding assistance
- Explain important product features
- Schedule an early customer check-in
- Provide tutorials and guidance
- Monitor customer activity during the first 30 days


3. MONTH-TO-MONTH CONTRACT CUSTOMERS

Month-to-month customers can be encouraged to move toward longer-term
contracts when appropriate.

Recommended actions:
- Explain benefits of long-term contracts
- Provide suitable contract incentives
- Offer personalized plans
- Communicate contract benefits clearly


4. HIGH MONTHLY CHARGES

Customers with high monthly charges may become dissatisfied if they do
not perceive sufficient value.

Recommended actions:
- Review the customer's current plan
- Explain available features
- Recommend a suitable plan
- Offer value-based incentives when appropriate
- Identify unused services


5. CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Poor customer support can increase customer dissatisfaction.

Recommended actions:
- Respond quickly to customer problems
- Provide clear solutions
- Follow up after resolving an issue
- Monitor repeated complaints
- Escalate serious issues when necessary


6. INTERNET SERVICE CUSTOMERS

Internet service customers should receive reliable service and
effective technical support.

If customers experience technical problems:
- Investigate the issue
- Provide troubleshooting assistance
- Escalate unresolved technical problems
- Follow up with the customer
- Monitor repeated technical issues


7. CUSTOMER ENGAGEMENT

Regular customer engagement can help identify dissatisfaction before
the customer decides to leave.

Recommended actions:
- Send useful product information
- Collect customer feedback
- Monitor customer activity
- Contact customers showing signs of dissatisfaction


8. RETENTION PRINCIPLE

Retention actions should be personalized according to the customer's
risk factors, tenure, contract type, service usage, and customer
history.

The goal is not simply to provide discounts. The goal is to identify
the customer's problem and provide the most appropriate solution.
